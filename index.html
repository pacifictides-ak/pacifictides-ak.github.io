Alaska & West Coast tides & marine directory

For anyone who works or plays on the water — recreational boaters and anglers, independent vessel owners and operators, commercial fishermen, company vessels and fleet operators, and coastal and maritime businesses. One useful page at a time: tide predictions from official sources, harbor and port links, local marine & shore directories. Fast, plain HTML. Not for navigation.

Verify before you go. Tide numbers here are copied from NOAA CO-OPS (U.S.) or, for British Columbia, from Canadian Hydrographic Service / tides.gc.ca products. Always double-check on the official site and check weather and marine notices.
