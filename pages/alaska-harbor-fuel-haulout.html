Petersburg, AK

Petersburg tides and full local directory

Harbor & harbormaster

Petersburg Borough Harbors
907-772-4688 · gwollen@petersburgak.gov · Website · 223 Harbor Way, Petersburg, AK 99833
The borough operates North, Middle, and South harbors plus a port dock, loading facilities, storage, launch ramps, and work floats. Harbor office main line and VHF Channel 16 (also CB-9). Staff cover shifts 24/7. Restrooms and a coin-operated public shower are available. Source: petersburgak.gov/harbor, checked 2026-11-07.

Dutch Harbor / Unalaska, AK

Dutch Harbor tides · Unalaska tides

Harbor & port authority

City of Unalaska Ports & Harbors (International Port of Dutch Harbor)
907-581-1254 · after-hours 907-359-1254 · portscheduling@unalaska.gov · Website · 748 Ballyhoo Road, Suite 201, Dutch Harbor, AK 99692
City facilities: Unalaska Marine Center Dock (including the USCG Dock), Carl E. Moses Boat Harbor, Robert Storrs Small Boat Harbor, the Spit Dock, and the Spit Light-Cargo Dock. Office Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; harbor officers staffed 24/7. No public VHF channel was listed on the official page when this was checked, so none is shown here. Source: unalaska.gov/portsandharbors, checked 2026-11-07.

Kodiak, AK

Kodiak tides and full local directory

Harbor & harbormaster

City of Kodiak Ports & Harbors (St. Paul / St. Herman)
907-486-8080 · harbor2@city.kodiak.ak.us · Website · 403 Marine Way / 710 Mill Bay Road, Kodiak, AK 99615
Two city marinas: St. Paul Harbor downtown (250 slips, 24' to 60') and St. Herman Harbor on Near Island (325 slips, 17' to 150'). Most slips have water and electricity. Office Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Harbor officers are on duty 24/7 and available after hours on VHF Channel 12 or 16, or at 907-654-8160. Source: city.kodiak.ak.us marina information, checked 2026-11-07.

Homer, AK

Homer tides and full local directory

Harbor & harbormaster

City of Homer Port & Harbor (Harbormaster)
907-235-3160 · port@cityofhomer-ak.gov · Website · 4311 Freight Dock Road, Homer, AK 99603
Homer small boat harbor on the Homer Spit, Kachemak Bay. Harbormaster office Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. (closed 12:30–1:00 for lunch; closed weekends). Harbor Officer on duty 24 hours, 7 days a week — hail on VHF Channel 16. City ice plant and fish dock: 382' face, −20' at MLLW, eight cranes; ice sold by the ton. Source: cityofhomer-ak.gov/port and Ice Plant & Fish Dock, checked 2026-11-08.

Northern Enterprises Boat Yard
