Petersburg tides and full local directory

Petersburg Borough Harbors
907-772-4688 · gwollen@petersburgak.gov · Website · 223 Harbor Way, Petersburg, AK 99833
The borough operates North, Middle, and South harbors plus a port dock, loading facilities, storage, launch ramps, and work floats. Harbor office main line and VHF Channel 16 (also CB-9). City of Kodiak Ports & Harbors (St. Paul / St. Herman)
907-486-8080 · harbor2@city.kodiak.ak.us · Website · 403 Marine Way / 710 Mill Bay Road, Kodiak, AK 99615
Two city marinas: St. Paul Harbor downtown (250 slips, 24' to 60') and St. Herman Harbor on Near Island (325 slips, 17' to 150'). Most slips have water and electricity. Homer, AK

Homer tides and full local directory

Harbor & harbormaster

City of Homer Port & Harbor (Harbormaster)
907-235-3160 · port@cityofhomer-ak.gov · Website · 4311 Freight Dock Road, Homer, AK 99603
Homer small boat harbor on the Homer Spit, Kachemak Bay. Harbormaster office Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. (closed 12:30–1:00 for lunch; closed weekends). Harbor Officer on duty 24 hours, 7 days a week — hail on VHF Channel 16. City ice plant and fish dock: 382' face, −20' at MLLW, eight cranes; ice sold by the ton. Source: cityofhomer-ak.gov/port and Ice Plant & Fish Dock, checked 2026-11-08.

Valdez, AK

Valdez tides and full local directory

Harbor & harbormaster

City of Valdez Harbors (Harbormaster)
907-835-4981 · harbor@valdezak.gov · Website · 300 N. Harbor Dr., Valdez, AK 99686
North Basin 489 slips and South Basin 127 slips, run by the City of Valdez, open seven days a week year-round. About 1,800' of transient dock in the North Basin and 1,400' in the South Basin (hot-berthing). Arriving by water: call the office on VHF Channel 16, then switch to working Channel 7. Office hours 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; summer (Memorial Day to Labor Day) 8 a.m.–7 p.m. Pump-outs at the ends of Tour and B docks (North) and V and R docks (South). Source: valdezak.gov Harbor, checked 2026-11-09.

Fuel docks

Crowley Fuels — Valdez (North & South harbor fuel docks)
907-835-4558 · Website · 105 Airport Road, Valdez, AK 99686
Crowley runs both fuel docks in the Valdez small boat harbor; seasonal dock hours are posted on its Valdez page.

Haul-out

City of Valdez Boat Yard
907-835-4981 · harbor@valdezak.gov · Website · 300 N. Harbor Dr., Valdez, AK 99686
75-ton marine travelift, eight 24' x 60' maintenance stations with water and power, all-tide launch ramp, fishermen's dock hoists, and a tidal grid for vessels up to 120' and 250 GT. Lifts are booked through the Harbor Office (source: valdezak.gov Boat Yard, checked 2026-11-09).

Whittier, AK

Whittier tides and full local directory

Harbor & harbormaster

City of Whittier Harbor (Harbormaster)
907-472-2327 · harbor@whittieralaska.gov · Website · P.O. Box 608, Whittier, AK 99693
A full-service, ice-free city harbor open year-round, with 360 slips for transient and permanent boats up to 60 feet. File float plans with the Harbor Office. Buy launch passes and parking at the office or in the Passport Parking app. Dry storage is available on request. VHF 16 and 68 are listed in the National Fisherman Pilothouse Guide. Harbormaster David Borg, ext. 244. Every road trip in or out goes through the Anton Anderson Memorial Tunnel, so check the DOT&PF tunnel schedule. Source: whittieralaska.gov Whittier Harbor, checked 2026-11-10.

Fuel docks

Shoreside Petroleum — Whittier (harbor fuel dock)
907-472-2314 · info@shoresidepetroleum.com · Website · Harbor Loop Road #2, Whittier, AK 99693
Marine fuel dock and highway station at the small boat harbor. Gas, diesel, and propane (Greater Whittier Chamber listing and Shoreside location page, checked 2026-11-10).

Haul-out

Cliffside Marina — boat yard services
harbormaster@cliffsidemarina.org · Website · 100 West Camp Rd, Whittier, AK 99693
Private 99-slip HOA marina. Its facilities page lists haul and launch by hydraulic trailer (blocking included), boat storage, bottom paint, fiberglass, and electrical repair. Whittier has no published travelift, so contact the marina for availability (checked 2026-11-10).

Kenai, AK

Kenai tides and full local directory

Dock, launch & harbor contact

City of Kenai — City Dock & boat launch (Public Works)
907-283-8261 · publicworks@kenai.city · Website · 1591 Boat Launch Road, Kenai, AK 99611
City-owned dock on the Kenai River off Bridge Access Road, with 4 launch ramps, 238 trailer parking spaces, and a commercial dock with 3 cranes (not open to the public). Kenai has no slip harbor and no harbormaster. Public Works Director Lee Frey (907-283-8240) is staff liaison to the Harbor Commission. The city brochure warns that launching stops at low tide until the water rises, and that commercial boats have ramp priority during openings. Source: kenai.city City Dock and dock brochure, checked 2026-11-11.

Fuel

Doyle's Fuel Service (delivery)
907-283-7655 · doylesfuelservice@wbialaska.com · Website · 14223 Kenai Spur Hwy, Kenai, AK 99611
Kenai has no public fuel float listed at the City Dock. Doyle's sells and delivers on/off-road diesel, gasoline, and AvGas on a set area schedule (source: doylesfuelservice.com, checked 2026-11-11).

Haul-out

No published travelift or haul-out yard in Kenai. Boats are trailered at the City Dock ramps; the nearest travel-lift yards on our pages are in Homer (Northern Enterprises lists 200-ton and 70/75-ton lifts).

Cordova, AK

Cordova tides and full local directory

Harbor & harbormaster

City of Cordova Harbor (Harbormaster)
907-424-6400 · harbor@cityofcordova.net · Website · Cordova Small Boat Harbor, Cordova, AK 99574
A 727-vessel single-basin harbor with slips from 24 to 70 ft, seaplane moorage, two tidal grids (160 ft light-duty and 180 ft heavy-duty), two launch ramps, bilge-water collection, and pumpout. Staffed 7 days, 8 AM–5 PM, on VHF 16 (working channel 68). Transient boats check in at the harbor office on arrival. Source: cityofcordova.net Port and Harbor, checked 2026-11-12.

Fuel docks

Shoreside Petroleum — Cordova (commercial fuel dock)
907-424-3264 · Website · 101 Copper River Highway, Cordova, AK 99574
Shoreside's Cordova terminal (opened 2007) has a commercial fueling dock for marine vessels and an Essential One retail station. The city harbor page names Shoreside as the harbor's fuel supplier (Shoreside About Us and City of Cordova, checked 2026-11-12).

Haul-out

City of Cordova — 150-ton travel lift & shipyard
907-424-6400 · harbor@cityofcordova.net · Website
Unsheltered city yard for repairs and storage. Reserve at least 24 hours ahead, sign the haul-out agreement, and pay in advance. Owners supply blocking, tarps, and tools. Travel-lift rates on the 2025 fee schedule run $24–$27 per foot by length (checked 2026-11-12).
Cordova Outboard — trailer launch & haul-out
907-424-3220 · info@cordovaoutboard.com · Website · 211 Breakwater Ave, Cordova, AK 99574
Trailer launch and haul-out for boats up to about 35 ft, plus year-round boat storage. Larger boats go to the city travel lift (cordovaoutboard.com, checked 2026-11-12).
