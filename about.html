About

A small, fast directory for anyone who works or plays on the water along Alaska and the U.S. and Canadian West Coast — recreational boaters and anglers, independent vessel owners and operators, commercial fishermen, company vessels and fleet operators, and coastal and maritime businesses.

Coverage plan

We ship one useful page at a time. Build order: all of Alaska first (Southeast, Southcentral, Southwest / Aleutians / Bristol Bay, Arctic & Western), then Washington, Oregon, and California ports, then British Columbia. Folder path on this machine stays se-alaska-tides for continuity.

What this is

Plain HTML pages with one useful prediction set or link hub at a time. U.S. tide numbers come from the NOAA Center for Operational Oceanographic Products and Services (CO-OPS) public API. British Columbia pages will use Canadian Hydrographic Service / tides.gc.ca / IWLS API stations. We do not invent heights or times.

Disclaimer — not for navigation

How pages are made

A small Python script in tools/generate_tide_page.py pulls high/low predictions from api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov and writes a static HTML page. Each page lists its station id and sources. Station ids are verified against the NOAA MDAPI station list before use.
