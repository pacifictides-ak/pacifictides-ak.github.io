Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-05. Pacific Tides is a free marine directory. We do not sell personal information.

Email list

If you subscribe to the free weekly “tides & marine weather for your port” email, we collect your email address, optional port preference, and your consent checkbox. We use that only to send the weekly email and related service notices (for example, how to unsubscribe).

Form submissions are delivered through a free form relay (gopigeon) to the site operator at jlmiller99833@gmail.com. We will not sell, rent, or trade your email to advertisers or data brokers.

Newsletter sending

Weekly sends will use a free email service provider once configured (recommended: Buttondown free tier). Until that is connected, signups are stored via the form relay inbox only.

Directory listings

Business names, phones, and websites shown in the port directory come from public sources or from businesses that request a listing. Featured listings are paid advertising.

Analytics & ads

No third-party analytics or display ad scripts are installed yet. If we add Google Search Console, analytics, or AdSense later, this page will be updated first.
